## Limits of this pass

- This is a curated research list, not a claim to exhaust every arXiv category or every spelling of world modeling. Search-index delays and terminology variation can still leave undiscovered papers.
- Reference-only non-arXiv resources were checked selectively. Workshop-directory links were not automatically promoted to paper records.
- All original arXiv IDs were checked at the metadata level; the 1,122 original papers were not all reread in full, and all older external project/code links were not retested. Full text was inspected where identity or scope was unclear.
- Titles and descriptions summarize authors’ claims; no experiments or benchmark results were independently reproduced.
