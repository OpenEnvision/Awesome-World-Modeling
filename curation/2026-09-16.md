## Newly included work

| Paper | Original submission | Placement | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Learning to Predict Without Looking Ahead: World Models Without Forward Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.13038) | 2019/10/29 | 0.2 | Uses observational dropout during reinforcement learning to induce an internal world model that fills gaps in observations, without an explicit supervised forward-prediction objective. |
| [A Biologically-Inspired Dual Stream World Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.08035) | 2022/09/16 | 0.2 | Separates visual observations into context and content streams inspired by the medial temporal lobe, generating imagined trajectories after one exposure and supporting policy learning through Dyna-like updates. |
| [Mind the Model, Not the Agent: The Primacy Bias in Model-based RL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15017) | 2023/10/23 | 3.1 | Diagnoses overfitting to early data in world models and periodically resets model parameters to improve adaptation in MBPO and DreamerV2. |
| [Dream and Search to Control: Latent Space Planning for Continuous Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09832) | 2020/10/19 | 3.2 | Uses tree-based planning over learned latent dynamics for continuous-action control, bootstrapping policy learning and supporting planning at test time. |
| [ActionReasoningBench: Reasoning about Actions with and without Ramification Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.04046) | 2024/06/06 | Benchmarks | Diagnostic benchmark for fluent and state tracking, action executability, direct and indirect action effects, and numerical reasoning; evaluates components of symbolic world models rather than learned visual simulation. |
| [PlanBench: An Extensible Benchmark for Evaluating Large Language Models on Planning and Reasoning about Change](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.10498) | 2022/06/21 | Benchmarks | Uses automated-planning domains to test plan generation and reasoning about actions and state changes; a downstream planning diagnostic, not a direct measure of learned world-model fidelity. |

Dates above are original arXiv submissions, not conference years. World Model Resetting was accepted at ECAI 2024, ActionReasoningBench at ICLR 2025, and PlanBench at NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks, as stated on their arXiv pages. PlanBench and ActionReasoningBench are included as diagnostics for planning and symbolic state transitions; they do not directly measure learned visual-world-model fidelity.
